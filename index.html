About

I am an astrophysicist working on the physics of black holes and alternative compact objects and their observational signatures. My work sits at the interface of general relativity, high-energy astrophysics, computational physics and machine learning, with the goal of connecting theoretical models to horizon-scale observations from the Event Horizon Telescope (EHT) and future facilities such as the proposed space-VLBI mission Black Hole Explorer (BHEX).

I received my PhD in Astrophysics in June 2026 from the Tsung-Dao Lee Institute, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, working in the black hole astrophysics group of Prof. Yosuke Mizuno. During my PhD I developed analytical and numerical modules for general relativistic magnetohydrodynamics (GRMHD), general relativistic radiative transfer (GRRT) and machine-learning applications, which are now used by several research groups.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Associate in Prof. Kazunori Akiyama's group within the BASP Lab (Institute of Sensors, Signals and Systems, led by Prof. Yves Wiaux) at Heriot-Watt University, Edinburgh, where I work at the meeting point of GRMHD/GRRT modelling, computational imaging and machine learning. I am a member of the EHT Collaboration.

Interests: black hole imaging & shadows · computational imaging · GRMHD / GRRT simulations · accretion flows & jets · naked singularities and tests of gravity · machine learning for astrophysics · fundamental constants with white dwarfs

Research highlights

Testing gravity with black-hole shadows

A quantitative framework showing how next-generation, higher-resolution VLBI imaging can distinguish General Relativity from alternative theories of gravity. Featured on the cover of Nature Astronomy (January 2026) with a research briefing.

Uniyal et al. 2026, Nature Astronomy · Cover · Briefing

Naked singularities vs. black holes

GRMHD and GRRT simulations of accretion onto naked singularities, identifying dynamical and radiative signatures that set them apart from black holes in horizon-scale observations.

Uniyal et al. 2025, ApJ

A generalized Fishbone–Moncrief torus

A revisited equilibrium torus solution giving robust, numerically stable initial conditions for long GRMHD simulations beyond the Kerr spacetime; adopted by several international groups.

Uniyal et al. 2024, ApJ

Fundamental constants from white dwarfs

A machine-learning framework for Gaia white-dwarf data that tightens constraints on the variation of the fine-structure constant and the proton-to-electron mass ratio.

Uniyal et al. 2025, MNRAS
